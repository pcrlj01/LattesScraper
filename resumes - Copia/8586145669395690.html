Doutor (PhD) em Engenharia e Gestão Industrial pela Faculdade de Engenharia da Universidade do Porto (FEUP), com reconhecimento nacional duplo pelo Programa de Pós-graduação em Administração e Controladoria da Universidade Federal do Ceará (UFC) e pelo Programa de Pós-graduação em Engenharia de Produção da Universidade Federal de Pernambuco (UFPE). Possui formação complementar em Gestão de Projetos Tecnológicos pela Universidad de León (ULe) e em Empreendedorismo e Inovação pela Porto Business School (PBS) e pela Nova School of Business and Economics (Nova SBE). Atuando há mais de uma década nos ecossistemas de inovação do Brasil e da Europa, desenvolve projetos na interface entre inovação, educação e gestão, assessorando startups, médias e grandes empresas, fundações, organizações sociais e universidades. Atualmente, é Pesquisador Pós-Doutoral da FGV EAESP no CEPID BRIDGE, sediado na FEA-USP, onde investiga o fenômeno de criação e orquestração de ecossistemas globais de inovação. Anteriormente, foi Pesquisador Pós-Doutoral em Internacionalização de Startups no Programa de Pós-graduação em Engenharia de Produção da Universidade Federal da Paraíba (UFPB) e Pesquisador Pós-Doutoral na Escola Superior de Agricultura Luiz de Queiroz da Universidade de São Paulo (ESALQ/USP), desenvolvendo pesquisas sobre internacionalização de startups e desenvolvimento de ambientes de inovação. Ao longo da carreira, atuou também como Gestor de Inovação e Empreendedorismo na Agência USP de Inovação (AUSPIN ESALQ), Professor convidado da disciplina PRG0005 Fundamentos em Empreendedorismo, a maior disciplina de empreendedorismo da USP, e Líder-fundador do laboratório de inovação onze19, voltado à amplificação da inovação em startups, médias e grandes empresas. Seus resultados acumulados incluem mais de 100 ideias, startups, empresas e projetos de inovação acelerados; mais de 30 desafios de inovação descobertos, validados e conectados com provedores tecnológicos; mais de 30 palestras, workshops e capacitações em inovação ministrados; participação e estruturação em mais de 20 eventos de inovação, como demodays, pitchdays e conferências; mais de 10 hubs digitais de inovação comercializados, implementados e/ou gerenciados; e impacto direto em mais de 20 organizações, fundações corporativas, médias e grandes empresas. Development and Internationalization of Startups from ESALQ
Descrição: To ensure that Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) fostered within the departments of ESALQ thrive in the global market as startups, it is imperative to cultivate an open innovation environment and to acquire expertise in expanding operations beyond Brazil. The primary local innovation hub, the Piracicaba Technological Park, is currently constrained by deficiencies in human capital, financial resources, infrastructure, and other key areas. These limitations hinder its capacity to adequately support the development of startups originating from the ESALQ community, as well as those emerging throughout Piracicaba Valley (AgTech Valley). Therefore, the development of a technological park for ESALQ is urgent. This development, to be successful, needs in its strategic planning to understand, in order to meet, the expectations of all stakeholders that make up the local and regional innovation ecosystem. Hence, the establishment of a technological park for ESALQ is imperative. However, for this endeavor to succeed, comprehensive strategic planning is essential. It must encompass a thorough understanding of, and alignment with, the expectations of all stakeholders within the local and regional innovation ecosystem. Conversely, ESALQ startups aspiring to penetrate the global agricultural market must proactively comprehend the barriers impeding their internationalization efforts. By gaining such insights, they can effectively anticipate challenges and strategize accordingly. Considering the challenges at hand, this research aims to achieve two primary objectives: (a) Identify and understand the diverse needs of stakeholders in the AgTech Valley regarding the establishment of an optimal open innovation ecosystem within ESALQ's Areão Farm, and (b) Uncover and analyze the obstacles hindering the international expansion of startups. To achieve these objectives, we will conduct two data collection initiatives in the form of semi-structured interviews, employing a multi-stakeholder qualitative research methodology. The first phase will involve interviewing key stakeholders from the AgTech Valley to gain insights into their requirements and anticipations regarding the establishment of an ESALQ technological park. The second phase will involve consulting key stakeholders within the Brazilian startup ecosystem. Sampling will continue until data saturation is reached, determining the endpoint of interviews. Theoretical frameworks pertaining to technological park development and barriers to internationalization will guide the analysis and categorization of collected data. The findings of the research will be disseminated globally through impactful articles and locally within the ESALQ community and the AgTech Valley through educational and collaborative initiatives. It is anticipated that the insights generated by this research will inform the planning of the new ESALQ innovation environment and expedite the internationalization efforts of startups from both the AgTech Valley and Brazil on a broader scale..

Barriers to Brazilian Born Global Start-ups Internationalization
Descrição: This postdoctoral research project investigates the barriers faced by Brazilian "Born Global" Startups (BBGSs) in their international expansion, a particularly relevant challenge in the post-pandemic context and the rapid growth of deep tech startups. Given that BBGSs must enter international markets from the start of their operations, understanding these barriers is crucial for their survival. The project aims to identify and analyze these barriers, providing valuable insights for new and existing Brazilian startups seeking to internationalize their operations, as well as supporting the countrys innovation ecosystem.The research employs a case study design and involves interviews with multiple stakeholders, including entrepreneurs, international accelerator leaders, specialized researchers, internationalization support agencies, and investors. The qualitative analysis of the data will be based on a theoretical framework of barrier categories proposed by Leonidou (1995a; 1995b). Expected outcomes include the identification of patterns and peculiarities of barriers according to the type of startup and international destination, the publication of a high-impact article, and the facilitation of knowledge transfer workshops for the national innovation ecosystem..

Development and Internationalization of Startups from ESALQ
Descrição: To ensure that Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) fostered within the departments of ESALQ thrive in the global market as startups, it is imperative to cultivate an open innovation environment and to acquire expertise in expanding operations beyond Brazil. The primary local innovation hub, the Piracicaba Technological Park, is currently constrained by deficiencies in human capital, financial resources, infrastructure, and other key areas. These limitations hinder its capacity to adequately support the development of startups originating from the ESALQ community, as well as those emerging throughout Piracicaba Valley (AgTech Valley). Therefore, the development of a technological park for ESALQ is urgent. This development, to be successful, needs in its strategic planning to understand, in order to meet, the expectations of all stakeholders that make up the local and regional innovation ecosystem. Hence, the establishment of a technological park for ESALQ is imperative. However, for this endeavor to succeed, comprehensive strategic planning is essential. It must encompass a thorough understanding of, and alignment with, the expectations of all stakeholders within the local and regional innovation ecosystem. Conversely, ESALQ startups aspiring to penetrate the global agricultural market must proactively comprehend the barriers impeding their internationalization efforts. By gaining such insights, they can effectively anticipate challenges and strategize accordingly. Considering the challenges at hand, this research aims to achieve two primary objectives: (a) Identify and understand the diverse needs of stakeholders in the AgTech Valley regarding the establishment of an optimal open innovation ecosystem within ESALQ's Areão Farm, and (b) Uncover and analyze the obstacles hindering the international expansion of startups. To achieve these objectives, we will conduct two data collection initiatives in the form of semi-structured interviews, employing a multi-stakeholder qualitative research methodology. The first phase will involve interviewing key stakeholders from the AgTech Valley to gain insights into their requirements and anticipations regarding the establishment of an ESALQ technological park. The second phase will involve consulting key stakeholders within the Brazilian startup ecosystem. Sampling will continue until data saturation is reached, determining the endpoint of interviews. Theoretical frameworks pertaining to technological park development and barriers to internationalization will guide the analysis and categorization of collected data. The findings of the research will be disseminated globally through impactful articles and locally within the ESALQ community and the AgTech Valley through educational and collaborative initiatives. It is anticipated that the insights generated by this research will inform the planning of the new ESALQ innovation environment and expedite the internationalization efforts of startups from both the AgTech Valley and Brazil on a broader scale..

Barriers to Brazilian Born Global Start-ups Internationalization
Descrição: This postdoctoral research project investigates the barriers faced by Brazilian "Born Global" Startups (BBGSs) in their international expansion, a particularly relevant challenge in the post-pandemic context and the rapid growth of deep tech startups. Given that BBGSs must enter international markets from the start of their operations, understanding these barriers is crucial for their survival. The project aims to identify and analyze these barriers, providing valuable insights for new and existing Brazilian startups seeking to internationalize their operations, as well as supporting the countrys innovation ecosystem.The research employs a case study design and involves interviews with multiple stakeholders, including entrepreneurs, international accelerator leaders, specialized researchers, internationalization support agencies, and investors. The qualitative analysis of the data will be based on a theoretical framework of barrier categories proposed by Leonidou (1995a; 1995b). Expected outcomes include the identification of patterns and peculiarities of barriers according to the type of startup and international destination, the publication of a high-impact article, and the facilitation of knowledge transfer workshops for the national innovation ecosystem..
