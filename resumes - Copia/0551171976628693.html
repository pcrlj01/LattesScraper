Projeto de Cooperação Internacional: Desafios dos sistemas elétricos frente à transição energética: cooperação internacional com foco na América Latina e Caribe
Descrição: Este projeto tem como objetivo estudar e desenvolver modelos e ferramentas computacionais para análise e mitigação de impactos em sistemas elétricos de potência, associados a cenários de transição energética. Entre esses cenários, pode-se destacar o aumento da penetração de recursos energéticos distribuídos nas redes elétricas, a evolução tecnológica que implica em mudanças nos perfis da carga a ser atendida, bem como novas possibilidades de marcos regulatórios, envolvendo transações energéticas entre unidades de consumo e/ou geração. O projeto prevê contribuições nos modelos citados visando incluir a representação de novas premissas advindas da transição energética já em curso e de cenários futuros. Esses modelos englobam ferramentas de planejamento da expansão e/ou operação, otimização e análise estatística/probabilística, vislumbrando: i) levantamento e tratamento de dados; ii) aplicação de técnicas de planejamento; iii) análise de resultados subsidiada pelo estado da arte e métricas de avaliação já consolidadas para a temática. Para isso, são apresentados quatro subprojetos considerando diferentes aspectos do planejamento da expansão e operação de sistemas elétricos, além de mercados de energia elétrica. São esperados como resultados que o projeto possa dar suporte a marcos regulatórios e novos procedimentos, que possam incorporar de forma mais adequada os requisitos da transição energética, através da ampliação da rede de pesquisa internacional de especialistas nos tópicos abordados..

National Institute of Electric Energy - INERGE
Descrição: The National Institute of Electric Energy (INERGE) was created in 2009 within policy of the foundation of the National Institutes of Science and Technology (INCTs) by CNPq, with the participation of FAPEMIG. Headquartered in the Federal University of Juiz de Fora (UFJF), the INERGE was created as a research network having as initial partners theFederal University of Juiz de Fora (UFJF), Federal University of São João Del-Rei (UFSJ), Federal University of Itajubá (UNIFEI), Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ) and Federal Fluminense University (UFF). The National Institute of Electric Energy has the following main objectives: to look for the excellence in the activities of science and technology in order to reach international level; to interact with the business sector and to improve technological education in Brazil. Specifically, the INERGE develops science and technology innovation for the benefit of the electric energy sector and its corresponding supply chain, making efforts to become a national and international leader in the development of products and processes with high technological value to the production, transmission and distribution of electric energy, to meet and anticipate the demands of the electric energy sector. This proposal, in response to the call INCT- MCTI / CAPES / FAPES 16/2014, seeks to develop new actions to encourage effective consolidation of INERGE within the national and international scenarios, as a research network of excellence in the area of electric energy. For this purpose, the project is divided into two major parts: (a) Description of INERGE Institute and (b) Description of RD Project. From the first part, the functional and organizational structure of INERGE is presented where the mechanism for high level education and its network integration is highlighted and the proposition of an International Ph.D. Program is proposed. From the second part, the integration of RD projects among the partner institutions is presented. Due to the success of INERGE initiatives to develop RD project related to Smart Grids (SG), in this 2nd term, the INERGE aims at focusing on developing researches, products and processes within the context of Smart Grid. This issue alone is multidisciplinary and multi-institutional and represents the major challenge for the next decades, in the area of electricity, that is, it is a theme at the frontier of knowledge, which requires new methodologies and products for smart operation, protection, coordination, control, and analysis of electric power systems. In this sense, this proposal is fully adherent to the strategic topics considered in the present public call, falling under the following themes: a) Alternative sources of renewable energy, biofuels and bioenergy; b) Information technology and communication.
